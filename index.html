Common questions

Do you sell motors, pumps or switchgear?

No. We only repair and service what you already own.

How long does a motor rewind take?

Small motors can often be done the same or next day. Larger motors take longer. We confirm the time when we quote.

Is rewinding better than buying new?

Usually for medium and large motors, yes. For very cheap small motors we will tell you honestly if replacing makes more sense.

Do you visit the site?

Yes, for panels, wiring, generators and equipment that can't be moved. Motors and pumps can be picked up.

Do you give a warranty?

Ask us when you call. Warranty terms depend on the job.
